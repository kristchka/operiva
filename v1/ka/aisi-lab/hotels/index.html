Bookings, payments and sales channels

Bookings

The assistant answers about available rooms and terms and guides the guest to a confirmed booking

Payments

Helps the guest pay according to hotel rules. Refunds and exceptions are decided by a person.

Sales channels

Brings requests from the channels where the hotel sells rooms into one history

Specific connections (property management systems, payment services, sales channels) and available actions are verified for each hotel separately

The assistant’s personality

Name, communication style, knowledge base and digital image are configured for each property. The assistant speaks in your hotel’s voice, not a template bot’s.

How it works

The dialogues below are examples written for demonstration. They are not recordings of real conversations.

An extra blanket

დიალოგის მაგალითი
Could I get another blanket in 204?
Of course. I’m passing your request to housekeeping, a blanket will be brought to room 204. Anything else?
Request created: housekeeping, room 204

Room availability

დიალოგის მაგალითი
Do you have a double room from 14 to 16 November?
Let me check availability for those dates. How many guests, and would you like breakfast?
Availability is checked against hotel data

House rules

დიალოგის მაგალითი
What time is check-in, and can I bring my dog?
I answer according to hotel rules: check-in time and pet policy are in the property’s knowledge base.

An unusual request

დიალოგის მაგალითი
I want to set up a surprise proposal in the room. Can you help?
What a lovely idea! Requests like this are best arranged with the front desk. I’m passing it on, and they’ll get in touch.
Handed to the front desk

Verification and limitations

We publish metrics only with the date, conditions, scope of the check and source, and always state where the check took place

Metrics will be added once the measurement data is agreed. Until then we do not publish figures.
  • Refunds, compensation and exceptions are decided by staff, not the assistant
  • Answers are based on the hotel’s knowledge base: if it is incomplete, the assistant hands the question to a person
  • Supported languages and connections are determined for each property
