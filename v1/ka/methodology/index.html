Methodology

Step-by-step implementation, from one scenario to scale

We don’t start with promises. First we study the process, agree on a result metric together, and expand only after the pilot has confirmed it.

  1. 1

    Study the task and process

    We look at how the process works now: channels, roles, systems, bottlenecks

    What we need from youDescribe the process and assign someone who knows it
  2. 2

    Choose the first scenario

    We choose one scenario where the benefit is visible and the risks are clear

    What we need from youAgree on the scenario and its limits
  3. 3

    Agree on the result metric

    We agree on what and how we measure, and record the baseline

    What we need from youGive access to data for the baseline or help collect it
  4. 4

    Knowledge and connections

    We prepare the knowledge base, rules, roles and system connections

    What we need from youProvide materials, rules and access; appoint an owner
  5. 5

    Pilot and verification

    We run the scenario at limited scope and compare it with the baseline

    What we need from youTake part in reviewing answers and give feedback
  6. 6

    Operation and support

    We move the scenario into regular work, monitor quality and update knowledge

    What we need from youTell us about changes in rules and processes
  7. 7

    Expansion

    We add roles and processes, only after a confirmed result

    What we need from youDecide on the next scenario
