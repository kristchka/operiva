Governance

You decide what AI can and cannot do

Digital employees work within limits set by people. Here is how rights, approvals, action history and responsibility work.

01

Roles and access rights

Each employee and digital role has its own set of rights. Data access is granted per task.

02

Allowed and forbidden actions

For each role we record the actions it performs on its own and the actions it is not allowed to take.

03

Human approval

Decisions with financial, legal or reputational consequences are carried out only after a staff member approves them.

04

Handing over complex cases

If a question goes beyond the rules or the AI is unsure, the request is handed to staff along with the conversation history.

05

Knowledge base management

Your team decides what the assistant knows: adding, correcting and removing materials.

06

Action history

Digital employees’ actions are recorded so the manager can check what was done and when.

07

Data principles

Data is used for the task it was provided for. The data owner can view, correct and delete it.

08

Responsibilities

Responsibility for data, settings and decisions is set out in the contract for each project.

We do not claim certifications or full legal compliance in specific countries until they are confirmed. We discuss the requirements of your industry and country before the project starts.
