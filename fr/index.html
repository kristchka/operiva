Data and trust

The principles behind our products

Data belongs to the person

People share information about themselves for a specific task and keep their rights to it.

Everything can be viewed, corrected and deleted

AI memory should be open to the data owner, not hidden inside the system.

Private stays private

Personal data is not used for other tasks or shared without consent.

A person decides the fate of their digital image

The owner decides who may access the image and when.

These are development principles. How each product implements them is verified separately and depends on its stage of readiness.
