Case

AISI LAB at the TiFLiS pilot hotel

The first property where AISI LAB works in a real hotel environment.

En service pilote
01
Property
TiFLiS is the pilot hotel where AISI LAB is being verified in real operation.
02
Starting point
Guest messages came through different channels, requests were passed to staff manually, and the manager had to piece together the day’s picture on their own.
03
Scope
A guest assistant with a configured name, character and knowledge base; requests for departments; a manager dashboard.
04
Scenarios
Answers based on hotel rules, questions about rooms and availability, guest requests to departments, unusual questions handed to the front desk.
05
Human role
Staff fulfil requests and decide on exceptions, payments and unusual situations. The manager sees summaries and situations that need a decision.
06
Current status
Pilot. The system runs at the hotel; scope and connections are being refined during verification.
07

Confirmed results

Quantitative results will be published once the measurement data is agreed — with date, conditions and source. Tests on a test copy and operation at the hotel will be shown separately.

08

Limitations and next steps

  • Pilot results at one hotel do not automatically apply to other properties.
  • Connections to hotel systems are verified for each property separately.
  • Next steps: complete the pilot, record metrics, expand scenarios.
