Lab

We study memory, context and character in artificial intelligence

The AISI LAB laboratory studies how AI can remember, take context into account and keep a recognisable character — and turns this work into products.

Architecture

NAL — neural memory architecture

Prototype

NAL is the lab’s own development. It lets AI avoid starting every conversation from scratch: keep context, accumulate experience and connect new events with what it already knows.

  • The first version of NAL is used in AISI LAB.
  • NAL is not sold as a separate product — it is the foundation of the lab’s products.

We publish the general principle. The internal technical design is not disclosed.

Digital assistant

A voice assistant that remembers the context of conversations and takes the user’s habits and preferences into account.

  • Voice interaction.
  • Keeps conversation context.
  • Learns habits and preferences.
  • Customisable communication style.
  • The user chooses the assistant’s name.

Areas of use: smart home, phone, email, plans and expenses.

Connected to the Digital Legacy project: the assistant’s memory is the basis of a future digital image.

PrototypeSortie prévue: février 2027*

Translator

Live speech translation for people who speak different languages. It takes conversation context into account thanks to the NAL architecture.

  • Live speech translation.
  • Takes conversation context into account.
  • Runs on the NAL architecture.

Supported languages and translation speed will be published after testing.

PrototypeSortie prévue: mars 2027*

Digital legacy

Preserving a person’s life story, memories, habits and traits, and creating a digital image from materials the person provided themselves.

  • Life story, memories, habits and personal traits.
  • The digital image is built only from materials the person provides.
  • In the future, loved ones will be able to talk to the preserved image.
  • The person decides how and by whom the image is used.

A digital image is not a preserved consciousness and does not replace a living person. It is a way to preserve their memory.

* Les dates sont indicatives et peuvent évoluer selon les résultats du développement et des tests.
