Three levels of freedom

The hotel chooses how much AISI decides on her own — and raises the level when it is ready

L1

Advises

AISI picks an option and passes it to the administrator. A person approves, AISI records the decision and learns from it

L2

Runs operations

Creates bookings and closes rooms herself, reporting in chat and on the Dashboard. Discounts, refunds, bonuses and advertising are agreed with a person

L3

Works autonomously

Decides prices, discounts, upgrades, expenses and suppliers herself — based on competitors, season and demand, strictly by the hotel's rules

At every level AISI has no access to company accounts: she makes no payments, only tracks due dates and sends reminders. Many hotels start at L1 and move to L2 within a month

How onboarding works

The timeline depends on your data and connections

  1. 1

    Hotel questionnaire

    Positions, rules and job descriptions. From them AISI builds her rights matrix

  2. 2

    Data transfer and reconciliation

    Rooms, rates, guests and bookings are moved and checked together with your team

  3. 3

    Soft start

    AISI connects to your PMS and channel manager read-only: you keep booking as usual, and she sees everything

  4. 4

    Team training and pilot

    Staff learn to work with AISI in chat and on the Dashboard, the hotel chooses the level of freedom

  5. 5

    Full operation

    Bookings are run in the AISI Dashboard. She syncs them to your system — or you drop it altogether
