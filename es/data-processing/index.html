Data processing

What data the website collects and why

A short, plain account of what happens to the data you leave on this website.

Who processes the data
DG Solution LLC, Tbilisi, Georgia. The company behind the AISI LAB laboratory and the OPERIVA website.
Contact form
We receive your name, company (if given), contact details and task description. This data is used only to reply to your request and discuss cooperation.
Website chat
The chat currently works as a demo: messages are processed in your browser and are not sent anywhere. This section will be updated when delivery is connected.
Cookies and analytics
The website does not use advertising trackers. If we add analytics, this section will be updated before it is enabled.
Your rights
You can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, via the contact form.
Changes
We update this page whenever the way the website handles data changes.
