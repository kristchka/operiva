AI Workforce

A digital workforce for repetitive processes

A digital employee is AI assigned a specific role: it performs defined tasks in agreed systems and hands over to a person whatever falls outside its limits.

Roles

Sales

Tasks
Answers incoming requests, clarifies needs, prepares an offer from a template.
Systems
Website, messengers, CRM.
Result
Every request gets an answer and lands in the CRM with context.
Limits of autonomy
Does not change prices or promise discounts without a manager’s approval.

Support

Tasks
Answers common questions from the knowledge base, collects details of the issue.
Systems
Chat, email, knowledge base.
Result
Routine questions closed, complex ones handed over with a description.
Limits of autonomy
Does not promise deadlines or compensation — hands over to staff.

Bookings

Tasks
Checks availability, clarifies terms, guides the booking.
Systems
Booking system, sales channels.
Result
Booking completed or handed to staff with the guest’s details.
Limits of autonomy
Does not cancel bookings or change rates without approval.

Service

Tasks
Takes customer requests and turns them into tasks for departments.
Systems
Task system, staff messengers.
Result
A request with an owner and a status.
Limits of autonomy
Hands unusual requests to a person.

Reviews

Tasks
Collects reviews, drafts replies, highlights recurring issues.
Systems
Review platforms, internal surveys.
Result
Draft replies and an issue summary for the manager.
Limits of autonomy
Publishes a reply only after a staff member checks it.

Coordination

Tasks
Tracks request deadlines, reminds owners, passes tasks between departments.
Systems
Task system, messengers.
Result
Fewer tasks lost between departments.
Limits of autonomy
Does not reassign owners without rules agreed by the manager.

Reporting

Tasks
Gathers data from connected systems into regular summaries.
Systems
The client’s connected systems.
Result
A summary without manual data collection.
Limits of autonomy
Shows data and deviations; conclusions and decisions stay with the manager.

Systems are listed as typical. Which ones are connected in your project is decided during preparation; building a new connection is discussed separately.

Context passes between roles

Thanks to the NAL memory architecture, roles don’t lose history: if a request starts in sales and continues in bookings and service, each role sees what has already been said and done.
Sales→Bookings→Service→Reporting

The human role

  • People define the roles, rules and limits for digital employees.
  • Decisions with financial or reputational consequences are approved by a person.
  • Complex and unusual cases are handed to staff with full context.
  • The manager sees the action history and can stop a scenario.
